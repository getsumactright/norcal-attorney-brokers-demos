## Concept 01: Architectural Authority (`concepts/01-architectural-authority.html`)
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `--bg-canvas`: `#0B0F17` (Obsidian Slate)
  - `--bg-surface`: `#131A26` (Elevated Charcoal)
  - `--bg-well`: `#0E141F` (Deep Slate Well)
  - `--brass-primary`: `#D4B06A` (Burnished Brass)
  - `--brass-bright`: `#E6C687` (Antique Gold Highlight)
  - `--text-ivory`: `#F6F4EE` (Primary Readout)
  - `--text-parchment`: `#D0D6E0` (Secondary Prose)
  - `--brass-rule`: `rgba(212, 176, 106, 0.24)` (1px Ledger Hairline)
- **Typography:** `EB Garamond` (Display & Section Headings) + `Hanken Grotesk` (Body, Tables & Controls), `tabular-nums` on all statutory and license identifiers.
- **Elevation & Geometry:** Sharp `2px`–`4px` stone-cut corners; planar `1px` brass hairlines without soft drop-shadow halos.

## Concept 02: NorCal Civic Modern (`concepts/02-norcal-civic-modern.html`)
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `--bg-travertine`: `#F6F2EA` (Warm Travertine Parchment)
  - `--bg-sandstone`: `#EBE4D6` (Sandstone Cream)
  - `--bg-alabaster`: `#FDFBF7` (Pure Alabaster Sheet)
  - `--redwood`: `#183629` (Capitol Redwood Green)
  - `--redwood-deep`: `#0F241B` (Deep Forest)
  - `--terracotta`: `#9C411F` (California Terracotta — WCAG AA verified against parchment/alabaster)
  - `--ink-espresso`: `#1A1917` (Primary Ink)
  - `--ink-stone`: `#46423B` (Secondary Stone Ink)
  - `--border-stone`: `#D5CBB8` (1px Natural Travertine Rule)
- **Typography:** `Libre Caslon Text` (Editorial Monograph Display) + `Figtree` (Humanist Geometric Body & Controls).
- **Elevation & Geometry:** `8px`–`12px` architectural contours; tonal surface layering over travertine parchment.

## Concept 03: Precision Deal & Litigation Dossier (`concepts/03-precision-dossier.html`)

  - `--white`: `#FFFFFF` (Dossier Cell Fill)
  - `--navy`: `#0E2A47` (Judicial Cobalt Navy)
  - `--navy-deep`: `#07192D` (Midnight Anchor)
  - `--vermilion`: `#C82D14` (Signal Vermilion Action Trigger)
  - `--ink`: `#121820` (Primary Ink)
  - `--slate`: `#364152` (Body Prose)
  - `--rule`: `#CFD6E0` (Exposed `1px` Structural Grid Line)
- **Typography:** `Bricolage Grotesque` (Sculptural Uppercase Display) + `Public Sans` (Utilitarian Body) + `JetBrains Mono` (CalDRE, State Bar, NMLS & Telemetry Notation).
- **Elevation & Geometry:** `0px` squared Swiss drafting cells locking flush with collapsing `1px solid #CFD6E0` borders.
