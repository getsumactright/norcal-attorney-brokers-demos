## Concept 01: Architectural Authority (`concepts/01-architectural-authority.html`)
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `--bg-canvas`: `#0B0F17` (Obsidian Slate)
  - `--bg-surface`: `#131A26` (Elevated Charcoal)
  - `--bg-well`: `#0E141F` (Deep Slate Well)
  - `--brass-primary`: `#D4B06A` (Burnished Brass)
  - `--brass-bright`: `#E6C687` (Antique Gold Highlight)
  - `--text-ivory`: `#F6F4EE` (Primary Readout)
  - `--text-parchment`: `#D0D6E0` (Secondary Prose)
  - `--brass-rule`: `rgba(212, 176, 106, 0.24)` (1px Ledger Hairline)
- **Typography:** `EB Garamond` (Display & Section Headings) + `Hanken Grotesk` (Body, Tables & Controls), `tabular-nums` on all statutory and license identifiers.
- **Elevation & Geometry:** Sharp `2px`–`4px` stone-cut corners; planar `1px` brass hairlines without soft drop-shadow halos.
