01 · Architectural Authority (Dark Editorial Luxury)

The Sovereign Counsel. Deep obsidian slate, elevated charcoal folios, and burnished brass 1px ruled ledger hairlines. Positions NorCal Attorney Brokers as an unassailable courtroom litigation and private wealth real estate powerhouse.

Signature UX: Interactive Legal vs. Brokerage Mandate Switcher in the hero, a 6-pillar interactive legal clause ledger that pre-fills the consultation form, and a brass-framed California Attorneys Oath chamber.

02 · NorCal Civic Modern (Warm Architectural)

The Capitol & Homestead Standard. Sun-washed travertine parchment, sandstone cream, Capitol redwood green, and California terracotta. Warm, welcoming, and deeply rooted in Greater Sacramento real estate and homeowner protection.

Signature UX: Panoramic Sacramento Capitol & Founder spread, an interactive 4-option "How Can Our Counsel Assist You?" pathway selector, a Two-Wing Law & Brokerage comparison, and category-filterable client stories.

03 · Precision Deal & Litigation Desk (Swiss Grid)

The Dual-License Dossier. Crisp architectural drafting paper, pure white cells, judicial cobalt navy, signal vermilion action triggers, and exposed 1px structural hairlines inspired by Swiss legal briefs and escrow dossiers.

Signature UX: First-viewport Interactive Matter Triage Desk, filterable 12-cell Practice Matrix where visitors click [+ Attach to Intake] to pre-populate their case file, and a 4-column licensed principals roster.
