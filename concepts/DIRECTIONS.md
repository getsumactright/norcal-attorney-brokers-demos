## 2. Direction Breakdown & Trade-Offs

### Direction 01 — Architectural Authority (*"The Sovereign Counsel"*)
- **Google Stitch Project:** `projects/10155096222003642632` ([01-architectural-authority-DESIGN.md](file:///Users/blackmountain/Claude%20Projects/NorCal%20Attorney%20Brokers/.stitch/01-architectural-authority-DESIGN.md))
- **Visual World:** Deep obsidian slate canvas with 1px burnished brass ruled ledger lines (`rgba(212, 176, 106, 0.24)`), mirroring the gold scales of justice in the firm's original emblem.
- **Why It Works:** Immediately elevates the firm out of template law-office territory into high-stakes private client advocacy. Ideal for attracting complex property litigation, quiet title/partition disputes, commercial transactions, and high-net-worth estate planning clients.
- **Trade-Off:** The commanding dark aesthetic feels formal and authoritative; visitors seeking casual residential home shopping may find Direction 02 warmer.

### Direction 02 — NorCal Civic Modern (*"The Capitol & Homestead Standard"*)
- **Google Stitch Project:** `projects/12911883184295281537` ([02-norcal-civic-modern-DESIGN.md](file:///Users/blackmountain/Claude%20Projects/NorCal%20Attorney%20Brokers/.stitch/02-norcal-civic-modern-DESIGN.md))
- **Visual World:** Sun-washed travertine parchment (`#F6F2EA`), sandstone cream (`#EBE4D6`), Capitol redwood green (`#183629`), and California terracotta (`#9C411F`), inspired by the firm's Sacramento State Capitol redwood banner (`assets/originals/hero-banner.png`).
- **Why It Works:** Balances institutional legal credibility with warm, community-oriented hospitality. Highlights Vanessa Amador's Sacramento roots and her inspiring transition from the 2007 foreclosure crisis into law school to protect homeowners.
- **Trade-Off:** Softer editorial pacing compared to the high-density legal matrix of Direction 03.

### Direction 03 — Precision Deal & Litigation Desk (*"The Dual-License Dossier"*)
- **Google Stitch Project:** `projects/10021131436364808810` ([03-precision-dossier-DESIGN.md](file:///Users/blackmountain/Claude%20Projects/NorCal%20Attorney%20Brokers/.stitch/03-precision-dossier-DESIGN.md) & [03-precision-dossier-thumb.png](file:///Users/blackmountain/Claude%20Projects/NorCal%20Attorney%20Brokers/.stitch/designs/03-precision-dossier-thumb.png))
- **Visual World:** Exposed `1px solid #CFD6E0` Swiss architectural grid on cool drafting paper (`#F4F6F9`), anchored by Judicial Cobalt Navy (`#0E2A47`) and Signal Vermilion (`#C82D14`) action triggers with monospaced statutory/license telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`).
- **Why It Works:** Built for decisive action and information clarity. Puts an interactive Matter Triage Desk in the first viewport and lets visitors attach any of the 12+ practice scopes directly to their consultation brief.
- **Trade-Off:** Clinical, structured brief aesthetic prioritizes speed and specificity over warm magazine storytelling.

## 3. Recommendation
- **For maximum warmth + dual Law/Brokerage balance:** Choose **Direction 02 (NorCal Civic Modern)** as the foundation, and bring in **Direction 03's** one-click `[+ Attach to Intake]` practice area pre-fill behavior.
- **For high-stakes litigation & commercial authority:** Choose **Direction 01 (Architectural Authority)**.
