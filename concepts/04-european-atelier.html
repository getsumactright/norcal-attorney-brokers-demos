Vanessa Amador, Esq.

Founder · Attorney, Broker & Loan Originator

DRE# 1384231 · BAR # 313407 · NMLS# 2138650
J.D., Lincoln Law School of Sacramento

Vanessa Amador is a Licensed Attorney, Broker, and Loan Originator with over 20 years of Real Estate experience. She began her Real Estate career in 2002 with the understanding that the purchase of a home is often one's largest and most important investment, one that should not be taken lightly. In 2007, Vanessa found herself in the foreclosure crisis where she quickly realized there was little help for homeowners. This realization compelled her to enter Law School, where she excelled and focused on those areas that protect the Homeowner.

Because Vanessa came to the legal profession from Real Estate Sales, there is an appreciation and understanding of both contracts and customer service. It is paramount that her Clients are aware of the status of the case and what to reasonably expect every step of the way. Raised in the Sacramento region (Del Campo High, Kennedy High, Elk Grove High) and educated at George Mason University and Lincoln Law School of Sacramento (J.D. 2015, CA Bar 2016), she defends clients in the courtroom while leading caution-minded owners through Contract Settlements, Landlord-Tenant Disputes, Estate Planning, Living Trusts, and Deed drafting.

Robert J. Anaya, Esq.

Senior Real Estate & Trial Litigator

California State Bar # 153195
30+ Years Law · 40+ Years Real Estate

With more than 30 years of legal experience and over 40 years in the real estate industry, Attorney Robert J. Anaya brings unmatched depth, precision, and personal commitment to every client he represents. Before entering private practice, Robert served as a Deputy District Attorney in Madera, Merced, and Del Norte Counties, successfully prosecuting a wide range of criminal matters and laying the foundation for his civil litigation practice.

After four decades as a licensed real estate broker—now retired—he has developed a sophisticated understanding of property law, title issues, and land-use matters. He has litigated and resolved complex disputes involving preliminary title report exceptions, quiet title actions, breach of contract, partition, specific performance, and eminent domain, and has served as a mediator for the California Association of REALTORS®, alongside bankruptcy protection and foreclosure defense.
