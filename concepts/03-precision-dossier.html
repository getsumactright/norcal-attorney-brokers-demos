Section 01 · Legal Capabilities

What Do Our Real Estate Attorneys Do?

Select any of our six core practice areas on the left to review how we protect your ownership rights, contracts, rental properties, and courtroom position.

Real Property Law & Ownership Rights

Real estate attorneys explain the different ways property can be owned—individually or jointly—and help ensure ownership rights are clearly defined and legally protected. We advise homeowners, co-owners, investors, and real estate professionals on statutory compliance and long-term risk management.

Property Ownership Agreements Structuring individual, joint tenancy, tenancy-in-common, and entity-held real property ownership.
Boundary & Easement Disputes Rights-of-way, easement acquisition and abandonment, parcel maps, subdivision maps, and surveys.
Broker Liability & Risk Counseling principals and brokers on California disclosure mandates and fiduciary duties.
Dual-License Advantage Evaluated through 20+ years of active California brokerage and 30+ years of legal practice.

Litigation & Courtroom Dispute Resolution

Real estate attorneys represent your interests in disputes and, when necessary, advocate for you in court to ensure you are treated fairly. We resolve complex disagreements between buyers, sellers, co-owners, and builders through trial advocacy, Ex-Parte motions, and mediation.

Quiet Title Actions Judicial proceedings to clear clouded title and establish clear, marketable ownership.
Partition Actions Representing co-owners, heirs, and partners in dividing or forcing the equitable sale of real property.
Breach of Fiduciary Duty Prosecuting and defending claims involving undisclosed defects and agent/partner breaches.
Specific Performance & Eminent Domain Enforcing purchase contracts in court and defending property owners in condemnation matters.

Landlord-Tenant Law & Unlawful Detainers

Real estate attorneys help ensure laws are followed for both landlords and tenants, protecting rights and supporting fair, enforceable rental agreements. We provide rapid courtroom representation and strategic notice guidance across Sacramento County.

Unlawful Detainer Representation Filing and defending Unlawful Detainer complaints with strict statutory compliance.
Emergency Ex-Parte Motions Rapid courtroom intervention to vacate improper filings and protect client records.
Lease Drafting & Review Preparing enforceable residential and commercial rental agreements under California law.
Notice & Eviction Strategy Advising property owners on 3-day notices and lawful resolution paths prior to filing.

Contract Law, Document Review & EMD Recovery

A real estate attorney reviews all documents for accuracy and ensures you fully understand the legal basis of the document. We protect buyers, sellers, and investors from signing unfavorable terms and fight to recover deposits when contracts are breached.

Purchase & Sale Review Line-by-line legal review of residential, commercial, and owner-finance agreements.
Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) Recovery Resolving escrow deposit disputes against home builders and defaulting counterparties.
Contract Workouts & Settlements Negotiating binding contract settlements to resolve defaults without prolonged litigation.
Disclosure Verification Auditing seller transfer disclosures and addenda to prevent post-closing liability.

Deed Drafting & Title Exception Resolution

Improperly prepared deeds can trigger unintended property tax reassessment or cloud marketable title. Our attorneys draft and record deeds with statutory precision and resolve preliminary title report issues before closing.

Grant & Quitclaim Deeds Drafting and recording inter-family, interspousal, and entity property transfers.
Preliminary Title Report Review Identifying and curing title exceptions, liens, and historical recording errors.
Trust Transfer Deeds Properly titling California real estate into Revocable Living Trusts.
County Recorder Map Analysis Reviewing parcel maps, subdivision maps, and records of survey for title accuracy.

Estate Planning & Living Trusts for Property Owners

The purchase of a home is often one's largest investment. We design Living Trusts, Wills, and succession structures that keep your real estate out of California probate court and protect generational wealth.

Revocable Living Trusts Comprehensive estate plans tailored for homeowners and multi-property investors.
Wills & Incapacity Directives Coordinating pour-over wills, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives.
Real Property Trust Funding Preparing and recording the deeds required to fund your real estate into your trust.
Probate & Successor Guidance Advising families and successor trustees on managing and selling trust real estate.

Because Vanessa came to the legal profession from Real Estate Sales, there is an appreciation and understanding of both contracts and customer service—ensuring clients always know the status of their case and what to reasonably expect every step of the way.
