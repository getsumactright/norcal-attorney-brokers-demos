Vanessa Amador, Esq. — From the 2007 Foreclosure Crisis to Homeowner Advocate

Licensed Attorney, Real Estate Broker & Loan Originator · DRE# 1384231 · BAR # 313407 · NMLS# 2138650

Vanessa Amador is a Licensed Attorney, Broker, and Loan Originator with over 20 years of Real Estate experience. She began her Real Estate career in 2002 with the understanding that the purchase of a home is often one's largest and most important investment. In 2007, Vanessa found herself in the foreclosure crisis where she quickly realized there was little help for homeowners. This realization compelled her to enter Law School, where she excelled and focused on those areas that protect the Homeowner.

Because Vanessa came to the legal profession from Real Estate Sales, she brings a rare appreciation of both contracts and customer service—ensuring clients always know the status of their case and what to reasonably expect every step of the way. Having grown up in the Sacramento region (attending Del Campo High School in Fair Oaks, Kennedy High School in Greenhaven/Pocket, and graduating from Elk Grove High School) before attending George Mason University and graduating from Lincoln Law School of Sacramento in 2015, she serves clients locally, in surrounding counties, and by referral out of State.

Robert J. Anaya, Esq.

Senior Litigator & Real Estate Counsel

CALIFORNIA STATE BAR # 153195

With more than 30 years of legal experience and over 40 years in the real estate industry, Attorney Robert J. Anaya brings unmatched depth and precision. Before private practice, Robert served as a Deputy District Attorney in Madera, Merced, and Del Norte Counties, laying the foundation for his formidable civil trial skills.

After four decades as a licensed real estate broker—now retired—and service as a mediator for the California Association of REALTORS®, he litigates complex disputes involving preliminary title report exceptions, quiet title, breach of contract, partition, specific performance, eminent domain, easements, and county recorder maps, alongside personal injury, bankruptcy, and foreclosure protection.
