Real Estate Law & Property Ownership

Real estate attorneys explain the different ways property can be owned—individually or jointly—and help ensure ownership rights are clearly defined and legally protected. From preliminary title report exceptions to broker liability and risk management, we safeguard your position at every stage.

Property Ownership Agreements Structuring individual, joint tenancy, tenancy-in-common, and entity-held real property ownership rights.
Broker Liability & Risk Management Advising property principals and real estate professionals on statutory disclosure duties and fiduciary compliance.
Boundary, Easement & Land-Use Structural and non-structural rights-of-way, easement acquisition and abandonment, parcel maps, and records of survey.

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

When negotiations require courtroom authority, our attorneys represent your interests in disputes and advocate for you in court to ensure you are treated fairly. We resolve complex disagreements between buyers, sellers, co-owners, and builders through strategic litigation, Ex-Parte motions, and mediation.

Quiet Title & Partition Actions Resolving contested property ownership, clearing clouded title exceptions, and litigating co-owner partition actions.
Breach of Fiduciary Duties Prosecuting and defending claims involving undisclosed defects, failure to disclose, and agent/broker fiduciary breaches.
Specific Performance & Eminent Domain Enforcing real property contracts, earnest money deposit (EMD) recovery against builders, and condemnation defense.

Landlord-Tenant Law & Unlawful Detainers

Real estate attorneys help ensure California laws are followed for both landlords and tenants, protecting rights and supporting fair, enforceable rental agreements. We handle 3-day notices, Unlawful Detainer complaints, Ex-Parte motions to vacate improper filings, and complex rental disputes.

Unlawful Detainer Representation Strategic eviction guidance, statutory notice compliance, and rapid Ex-Parte courtroom defense in Sacramento County.
Enforceable Lease & Rental Agreements Drafting and auditing residential and commercial leases to align with evolving California statutory mandates.
Early Dispute Resolution Evaluating legal options before filing to resolve tenant or landlord conflicts efficiently and protect your record.

Contract Law & Document Review

A real estate attorney reviews all documents for accuracy and ensures you fully understand the legal basis of the document. Because our firm bridges real estate sales and legal practice, we spot transactional pitfalls before you sign and negotiate binding contract settlements when disputes arise.

Purchase & Sale Agreements Drafting and reviewing commercial, residential, and owner-finance contracts to protect your financial interests.
Breach of Contract Settlements Negotiating contract workouts, seller/buyer default remedies, and escrow deposit releases without unnecessary litigation.
Comprehensive Document Review Line-by-line legal verification of addenda, seller disclosures, financing terms, and settlement statements.

Title & Deed Transfers

Improperly drafted deeds can trigger unintended reassessment, title clouds, or family disputes. We handle deed drafting, title searches, preliminary title report exceptions, and complex inter-family or entity property transfers across Northern California.

Deed Drafting & Recording Grant deeds, quitclaim deeds, interspousal transfers, and trust transfer deeds prepared with statutory precision.
Title Search & Exception Resolution Analyzing County Recorder parcel maps, subdivision maps, and curing preliminary title report exceptions prior to closing.
Quiet Title Remedies Judicial resolution when historical recording errors or prior liens cloud marketable title.

Vanessa Amador, Esq.

Founder · Attorney, Real Estate Broker & Loan Originator

Vanessa Amador is a Licensed Attorney, Broker, and Loan Originator with over 20 years of Real Estate experience. She began her Real Estate career in 2002 with the understanding that the purchase of a home is often one's largest and most important investment, one that should not be taken lightly. In 2007, Vanessa found herself in the foreclosure crisis where she quickly realized there was little help for homeowners. This realization compelled her to enter Law School, where she excelled and focused on those areas that protect the Homeowner.

Because Vanessa came to the legal profession from Real Estate Sales, there is an appreciation and understanding of both contracts and customer service. It is paramount that her Clients are aware of the status of the case and what to reasonably expect every step of the way.

Raised in the Sacramento region (Del Campo High School, Kennedy High School, Elk Grove High School) and educated at George Mason University and Lincoln Law School of Sacramento (J.D. 2015, CA Bar 2016), Vanessa excels in the courtroom defending and correcting the mistakes of the past while leading caution-minded clients through Contract Settlements, Landlord/Tenant Disputes, Estate Planning, Living Trusts, and Deed drafting.

Robert J. Anaya, Esq.

Senior Litigator & Real Estate Counsel

With more than 30 years of legal experience and over 40 years in the real estate industry, Attorney Robert J. Anaya brings unmatched depth, precision, and personal commitment to every client he represents. Before entering private practice, Robert served as a Deputy District Attorney in Madera, Merced, and Del Norte Counties, successfully prosecuting a wide range of criminal matters. This early courtroom experience laid the foundation for the strong litigation skills he now applies across his civil practice.

After four decades as a licensed real estate broker—now retired—he has developed a sophisticated understanding of property law, title issues, and land-use matters. He has litigated and resolved complex disputes involving preliminary title report exceptions, quiet title actions, breach of contract, partition, specific performance, and eminent domain, and has served as a mediator for the California Association of REALTORS®.

His broad legal background in personal injury, bankruptcy protection, and foreclosure defense allows him to identify issues that intersect with real estate—such as creditor claims, property division, or liability concerns—and provide comprehensive guidance.
