## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-stakes fiduciary precision, quiet wealth, and bespoke NorCal real estate advocacy. It bridges the gap between white-shoe private legal counsel and high-end boutique property brokerage for high-net-worth clients in Sacramento and Elk Grove.

### Visual Philosophy
- **Editorial Architectural Minimalism:** The visual language rejects SaaS tech trends in favor of bespoke architectural ledgers, fine-press legal journals, and private wealth folios.
- **Deep Obsidian Immersion:** Dark canvas environments create high-contrast gravity, focusing client attention on high-value contract terms, architectural assets, and legal credentials.
- **Burnished Tactility:** Metallic accents mimic foil stamping, warm brushed brass fixtures, and wax-seal signatures through low-opacity border trims, subtle sheen gradients, and hairline precision.
- **Restrained Authority:** Interactions are deliberate, weighted, and fluid, avoiding bouncy or overly casual animations in favor of calibrated ease transitions.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep low-reflection slate tones enriched by warm antique metallics and cool parchment typography.

### Color Tiers & Intent
- **Canvas / Base (`#0B0F17`):** Obsidian Slate. Serves as the primary dark field across viewports, establishing atmospheric depth.
- **Surfaces (`#131A26`):** Elevated Charcoal. Used for cards, dossier panels, dialogs, and structural columns.
- **Surface Highlight / Tonal (`#1E2738`):** Deep Navy-Slate. Used for inner card wells, interactive hover states, and input field backgrounds.
- **Primary Accent (`#C8A464`):** Burnished Brass. Core brand color for primary action surfaces, refined focus states, credential highlights, and key figures.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D4AF37`):** Antique Gold. Reserved for subtle metallic gradients, active states, active tab markers, and illuminated badges.
- **Hairline Borders (`rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.22)`):** Structural hairline dividers and card outlines reminiscent of hand-ruled ledger margins.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - Headings & Primary Readout: Ivory Cream (`#F6F4EE`) at 100% opacity.
  - Body & Longform Text: Muted Parchment (`#C5CCD6`) at 88% opacity.
  - Captions, Meta, & Footnotes: Deep Fiduciary Stone (`#7A8699`) at 65% opacity.

## Typography

The typography pairs classical literary gravitas with modern legibility:

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for structural narrative anchors, page titles, deed-tier headers, and editorial quotations. Rendered with tight negative letter tracking on large sizes to preserve classical proportions without feeling archaic.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings clean geometrical balance to legal disclosures, analytical real estate metrics, property specifications, and transactional flows.
- **Legal Indices & Ledger Meta:** Uppercase labels (`label-md` and `label-sm`) utilize generous letter spacing (`0.1em` to `0.14em`) to reference catalog numbering, bar admission credentials, and title record identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is rooted in a structured 12-column editorial grid that references print folios and legal broadsheets.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `gutter-lg` (2.5rem) and `margin-lg` (5rem). High use of asymmetrical layouts (e.g., 5-column editorial sticky dossier paired with 7-column property inventory or legal briefs).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin-md` (2.5rem). Secondary meta sidebars reflow to full-width horizontal accordions.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1.25rem). Strict linear stacking with edge-to-edge card divisions utilizing 1px brass dividers instead of full margin nesting.

### Rhythm
- Component interior padding relies on `space-md` (inputs, list items) to `space-lg` (cards, modal panels).
- Page sections maintain generous breathing room (`space-xl` or larger) to reflect luxury pacing and deliberative legal consultation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical sheet layering over deep space rather than stark dropshadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** `#0B0F17` canvas punctuated by localized subtle radial glows (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(30, 39, 56, 0.4) 0%, rgba(11, 15, 23, 0) 70%)`).
- **Elevated Ledger Panels (Level 1):** `#131A26` cards bounded by crisp 1px borders in `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.22)`. No heavy dropshadow is used; depth is established strictly by border luminosity against the darker canvas.
- **Overlays, Popovers, & Modals (Level 2):** Elevated `#131A26` surfaces flanked by a multi-tier ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.35)`.
- **Foil Sheen Accent:** Critical highlights (e.g., attorney seal emblems, primary callout badges) employ an angled linear gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C8A464 50%, #9B783E 100%)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness. Standard components use an intentional `2px` to `4px` corner radius (`roundedness: 1`), avoiding soft, bubbly UI tropes in favor of stone-masonry cuts and trimmed paper edges:

- **Base Cards & Modules:** 4px radius (`rounded-lg` token clamped to `0.25rem`–`0.375rem`).
- **Inputs, Buttons, & Badges:** 2px radius for razor-sharp edge precision.
- **Circular Elements:** Reserved exclusively for attorney portrait apertures, notary seal stamps, and status micro-dots.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Background in Burnished Brass (`#C8A464`), text in obsidian `#0B0F17` with bold label typography (`label-md`). Hover state transitions to Antique Gold (`#D4AF37`) with a subtle brass outer halo (`0 0 16px rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Fiduciary Outlined:** Transparent surface with 1px border `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.45)`, text in `#F6F4EE`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.08)` and border to `#C8A464`.
- **Ghost Action:** Text in `#C8A464` with an underline positioned 4px below baseline; underline animates width from 40% to 100% on hover.

### Badges & Credential Seals
- Compact rectangular forms with 2px radius. Background: `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.08)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.28)`. Text: `label-sm` in `#D4AF37` with uppercase tracking.
- Used for California State Bar numbers, DRE license status, MLS verified badges, and escrow transaction tiers.

### Cards & Property Folios
- Solid Elevated Charcoal (`#131A26`) background framed with 1px `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.22)`.
- Header areas feature EB Garamond titles above a hairline separator (`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.12)`).
- Card footers for metrics (e.g., Acreage, Cap Rate, Retainer Schedule) utilize a tabular ledger grid with subtle vertical dividers.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#0E141F`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.2)`. 
- Padding: 0.75rem 1rem. Text: `#F6F4EE`. Placeholder: `#7A8699`.
- Focus State: Border shifts to `#C8A464` with no thick rings; accompanied by a discrete 1px brass glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #C8A464`).
- Label: Rendered in `label-sm`, uppercase, positioned above the field with a muted tone (`#C5CCD6`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square markers (2px radius for checkboxes; circular for radios) measuring 16px x 16px.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.4)`. Unchecked background: transparent.
- Checked State: Solid `#C8A464` fill with `#0B0F17` checkmark icon or center pip.

### Ledger Lists & Transaction Schedules
- Alternating or bordered list rows with 1px horizontal dividers (`rgba(200, 164, 100, 0.1)`).
- Key-value pairs align metadata to the right in tabular numeric formatting (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`).
