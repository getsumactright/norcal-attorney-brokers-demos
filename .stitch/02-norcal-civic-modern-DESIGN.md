## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of bespoke legal counsel and premium Northern California property brokerage. The visual identity draws from civic modernism and coastal architectural warmth—blending the institutional weight of high-stakes real estate law with the sunlit, organic ease of Northern California travertine, sandstone, and old-growth redwoods.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** and **Tactile Architectural Editorial**:
- **Generous Whitespace:** Spacious margins and stately column layouts evoke high-end architectural monographs and legacy legal charters.
- **Understated Dignity:** Restrained ornamentation replaces loud digital patterns. Interactions rely on measured, graceful transitions, crisp typography, and quiet material layers.
- **Editorial Cadence:** Large-scale serif headlines pair with disciplined modern grotesque sans-serifs, evoking the tactile authority of signed documents and sunlit drafting tables.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in Northern California’s natural geology and civic institutions:
- **Primary Canvas (`#F6F2EA`):** Warm Travertine Parchment acts as the primary background, eliminating sterile digital whites in favor of sun-washed stone.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#EBE4D6`):** Sandstone Cream establishes card backgrounds, side panels, and elevated inset structures.
- **Primary Accent (`#183629` & `#11261D`):** Capitol Redwood Green brings authoritative institutional gravitas to primary actions, prominent badges, and navigation anchors, darkening to deep forest on hover and active states.
- **Warm Accent (`#B85D3B`):** California Terracotta introduces precise editorial inflection—used sparingly for key stat callouts, verification marks, active tabs, and delicate focus highlights.
- **Ink & Typography:** Deep Espresso Ink (`#1A1917`) provides high-contrast, publication-grade legibility for headlines and body text, while Muted Stone Charcoal (`#4A4741`) handles supporting metadata, subtitles, and captions.
- **Structural Dividers (`#D8CFC0`):** Warm Natural Border produces soft, tactile framing without harsh digital grid lines.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between traditional legal authority and contemporary architectural publishing.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display delivers classical proportion and typographic drama reminiscent of estate prospectuses and civic architecture monographs. Headlines use tight letter-spacing and generous line-heights to avoid crowding.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides crisp geometric clarity, ensuring property disclosures, contract terms, technical spec sheets, and dense transaction matrices maintain effortless readability.
- **Case Formatting:** Small labels and metadata tokens (`label-md`, `label-caption`) frequently employ uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror architectural blueprint specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetric 12-column editorial fluid grid bounded by a max container width of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `4rem` (64px) canvas margins. Section-level editorial layouts balance wide visual anchor panels (e.g., 7 or 8 columns) with dense listing data or legal stipulations (e.g., 4 or 5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Complex dual-column split spreads fold gracefully into stacked cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Secondary metadata collapses into clean accordion stacks.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Macro-sections utilize expansive spacing (`space-xl` and higher) to communicate calm confidence and room to breathe, avoiding cramped dashboard visual density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects simulated 3D drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** accented by razor-thin, tactile outlines and low-opacity, warm ambient diffusion:

- **Surface Layering:** Depth is conveyed by placing Sandstone Cream (`#EBE4D6`) cards directly over Travertine Parchment (`#F6F2EA`), framed with hairline `1px` borders in Warm Natural Border (`#D8CFC0`).
- **Warm Ambient Occlusion:** Floating or active overlay elements (dialogs, flyout dossiers, dropdown menus) use a warm, heavily diffused ambient shadow tinted with Deep Espresso: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(26, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Inlaid Depth:** Recessed sections, interactive inputs, and secondary data wells apply inset hairline borders (`border: 1px solid #D8CFC0`) without internal shadows, maintaining a pristine, drafted blueprint quality.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances architectural structure with refined touchpoints:
- **Containers & Cards:** Uniformly bounded with a deliberate `0.75rem` (12px) radius (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg` transition), delivering an intentional, crafted feel that feels neither harsh nor overly playful.
- **Buttons & Control Pills:** Form fields, interactive chips, and action buttons adopt a matching `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) contour, keeping interactive elements congruent with card framing.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** Compact categorization elements maintain slight curvature (`0.25rem` / 4px) or full circular pills for transaction state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Capitol Redwood Green (`#183629`) background with Parchment (`#F6F2EA`) text. Subtle transition to Deep Forest (`#11261D`) on hover. Height: 48px, horizontal padding: `space-lg`, border-radius: 8px.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a `1px` border in Warm Natural Border (`#D8CFC0`) and Deep Espresso Ink (`#1A1917`) text. On hover: shifts to Sandstone Cream (`#EBE4D6`).
- **Warm Accent Link:** California Terracotta (`#B85D3B`) text paired with an underline offset by 4px, transitioning to an organic arrow glyph motion on hover.

### Cards & Dossiers
- **Structure:** Sandstone Cream (`#EBE4D6`) background, `12px` border-radius, bounded by a crisp `1px` border (`#D8CFC0`). Padding: `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Editorial Card Variant:** Travertine background with an inset photo well, fine hairline divider lines, and Playfair Display title headings.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Sandstone Cream or Travertine background, `1px` border in `#D8CFC0`, `8px` corner radius, text in Deep Espresso Ink (`#1A1917`). Height: 44px. Placeholder in Muted Stone Charcoal (`#4A4741`) at 60% opacity. Focus state applies a `1.5px` border in Capitol Redwood Green (`#183629`) with zero harsh outer rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted square (4px radius) and round selectors with a `1.5px` border in `#D8CFC0`. Checked state fills with Capitol Redwood Green (`#183629`) holding a crisp parchment tick.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Transaction & Legal Status:** Pill or 4px radius chips. Off-market/Exclusive status utilizes California Terracotta tint (`rgba(184, 93, 59, 0.12)`) with `#B85D3B` text. Verified/Under Contract status utilizes Capitol Redwood tint with `#183629` text.

### Architectural Listing & Provision Lists
- **Data Tables & Clause Lists:** Clean horizontal rows divided by `1px` `#D8CFC0` rules. Generous padding (`space-md` vertical), featuring `label-caption` uppercase category tags preceding the data values.
